## 📅 Week 1: Foundations
- **Day 1**: Read about **scarcity** and write 3 examples from your life.  
- **Day 2**: Study **opportunity cost**; practice by listing trade‑offs in daily choices.  
- **Day 3**: Learn basic **economic definitions** (goods, services, resources).  
- **Day 4**: Explore **production factors**: land, labor, capital, entrepreneurship.  
- **Day 5**: Watch a short video or read an article on **choices in economics**.  
- **Day 6**: Review and make **flashcards** for Week 1 terms.  
- **Day 7**: Write a **weekly summary** of what you learned.  

---

## 📅 Week 2: Microeconomics
- **Day 8**: Study **demand basics**.  
- **Day 9**: Study **supply basics**.  
- **Day 10**: Learn **market equilibrium**.  
- **Day 11**: Explore **elasticity** with simple examples.  
- **Day 12**: Compare **market structures** (competition vs monopoly).  
- **Day 13**: Apply microeconomics: track a local product’s price (e.g., maize).  
- **Day 14**: Write a weekly summary of microeconomics concepts.

## 📅 Week 3: Macroeconomics
- **Day 15**: Learn **GDP basics**.  
- **Day 16**: Study **inflation** and its effects.  
- **Day 17**: Study **unemployment** types.  
- **Day 18**: Explore **fiscal policy**.  
- **Day 19**: Explore **monetary policy**.  
- **Day 20**: Read a news article on Malawi’s economy and connect it to GDP/inflation.  
- **Day 21**: Write a weekly summary of macroeconomics concepts.  

---

## 📅 Week 4: Systems & Application
- **Day 22**: Study **economic systems**.  
- **Day 23**: Compare Malawi’s mixed economy with another country.  
- **Day 24**: Learn **international trade basics**.  
- **Day 25**: Explore **development economics**.  
- **Day 26**: Study **poverty and inequality**.  
- **Day 27**: Apply concepts: analyze how inflation affects households in Malawi.  
- **Day 28**: Write a final summary tying together micro, macro, and systems.

## 🎯 Study Method
- Spend **30–45 minutes daily**.  
- Use **flashcards** for memorization.  
- Relate each concept to **real-life examples** in Malawi.  
- End each week with a **summary reflection**.
