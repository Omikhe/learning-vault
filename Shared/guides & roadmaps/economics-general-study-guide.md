## 📖 Step 1: Core Principles

- **Scarcity**  
  Resources are limited, but human wants are unlimited. This is the root of all economic problems.  

- **Opportunity Cost**  
  Choosing one option means giving up another. Every decision has a trade-off.  

- **Supply and Demand**  
  Prices are determined by the interaction of buyers (demand) and sellers (supply).  

---

## 📊 Step 2: Branches of Economics

| **Branch** | **Focus** | **Example** |
|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Microeconomics** | Individuals, households, firms | Price of maize in Lilongwe |
| **Macroeconomics** | National/global economy | Malawi’s inflation rate |

---

## 💡 Step 3: Key Concepts to Learn

- **Markets**: Perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly.  
- **Economic Systems**: Market, command, mixed.  
- **GDP**: Measures the value of goods and services produced.  
- **Inflation**: Rise in prices over time.  
- **Unemployment**: Percentage of people without jobs but seeking work.  

---

## 🌍 Step 4: Malawi Context

- Agriculture drives much of the economy — maize, tobacco, and tea are key exports.  
- Village banks and microfinance illustrate microeconomic principles of savings and credit.  
- Government fiscal policy (taxes, spending) and monetary policy (interest rates, currency stability) shape macroeconomic outcomes.  

---

## 🎯 Step 5: How to Study Effectively

- Start with **basic definitions** before tackling complex theories.  
- Use **real-life examples** from Malawi’s economy to make concepts relatable.  
- Practice with **flashcards** for terms like GDP, inflation, elasticity.  
- Apply concepts to everyday decisions — budgeting, saving, or choosing between products.  

---

## 📌 Suggested Learning Path

1. **Scarcity and choices**

3. **Microeconomics basics**  
4. **Macroeconomics basics**  
5. **Economic systems**
