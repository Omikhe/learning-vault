## 🧩 Core Principles for Learning Math

- **Number sense**: Understand how numbers relate, not just memorize facts.
    
- **Spaced practice**: Spread learning over time instead of cramming.
    
- **Interleaving**: Mix problem types to strengthen flexibility.
    
- **Growth mindset**: Believe ability improves with effort.
    
- **Visual representations**: Use diagrams, graphs, and models to deepen understanding.
    
- **Real-world connections**: Apply math to everyday problems for retention.
    

## 📈 Step-by-Step Path

1. **Arithmetic basics**
    
    - Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division.
        
    - Practice mental math and estimation.
        
2. **Fractions and decimals**
    
    - Essential for algebra and higher math.
        
    - Use visual models (pie charts, grids).
        
3. **Pre-algebra**
    
    - Variables, simple equations, ratios, percentages.
        
    - Build comfort with abstract notation.
        
4. **Algebra**
    
    - Linear equations, inequalities, functions.
        
    - Foundation for advanced topics.
        
5. **Geometry**
    
    - Shapes, angles, area, volume.
        
    - Connect to spatial reasoning and design.
        
6. **Advanced topics**
    
    - Trigonometry, calculus, probability, statistics.
        
    - Progress only after mastering prerequisites.
        

## ⚠️ Common Barriers & Fixes

|**Barrier**|**Impact**|**Fix**|
|---|---|---|
|**Cumulative dependency**|Missing one concept blocks progress|Diagnose gaps with placement tests (e.g., Khan Academy)|
|**Abstract notation**|Symbols feel foreign|Translate symbols into plain language|
|**Math anxiety**|Reduces working memory|Practice low-stakes problems, build confidence|

## 🎯 Practical Tips

- **Daily short practice**: 20–30 minutes beats long cramming.
    
- **Talk through problems**: Explaining aloud improves retention.
    
- **Use free resources**: Khan Academy, YouTube math channels, open textbooks.
    
- **Join study groups**: Peer learning helps fill gaps.
