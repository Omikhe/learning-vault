## 📅 Week 1: Foundations of Economics
- **Scarcity** and **Opportunity Cost**  
- **Basic Definitions**: goods, services, resources, markets.  
- **Activity**: Write down 3 personal choices you made this week and identify the opportunity cost of each.  

---

## 📅 Week 2: Microeconomics Basics
- **Supply and Demand** and market equilibrium.  
- **Elasticity**: how sensitive demand/supply is to price changes.  
- **Market Structures**: perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly.  
- **Activity**: Track the price of a common product (like maize) in your area and analyze what factors might affect its demand and supply.  

https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/microeconomics

---

## 📅 Week 3: Macroeconomics Basics
- **GDP**: measuring national output.  
- **Inflation** and **Unemployment**.  
- **Fiscal Policy** vs. **Monetary Policy**.  
- **Activity**: Read a recent news article on Malawi’s inflation or GDP growth and summarize how it affects households.  

https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/macroeconomics

---

## 📅 Week 4: Economic Systems and Application
- **Types of Economic Systems**: traditional, command, market, mixed.  
- **Global Trade** and Malawi’s role in exports/imports.  
- **Development Economics**: poverty, inequality, sustainability.  
- **Activity**: Compare Malawi’s mixed economy with another country’s system and note key differences.  

---

## 🎯 Study Tips
- Use **flashcards** for key terms.  
- Relate concepts to **real-life examples** in Malawi (maize prices, mobile money, village banks).  
- At the end of each week, write a short **summary** of what you learned.
